Privacy Policy for Snake

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Snake mobile game ("the App"), available on the Apple App Store and Google Play, handles your information. In short: the App does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your high score locally on your device so it can be shown the next time you play. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites and makes no network requests during gameplay.

Third-party services

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Those platforms may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies, which we do not control:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
